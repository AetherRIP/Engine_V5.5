# Whole Math checker V5.5

This repository is part of the **Whole Math checker V5.5**.

It contains the correction-strategy benchmark and the teacher-certified holdout intake,
registration and promotion workflow:

- [benchmark/README.md](benchmark/README.md) — measured pilot, strategies and status.
- [benchmark/holdout/INTAKE_GUIDE.md](benchmark/holdout/INTAKE_GUIDE.md) — intake,
  certification, registration and promotion workflow.
- [benchmark/holdout/CERTIFICATION_PROTOCOL.md](benchmark/holdout/CERTIFICATION_PROTOCOL.md) —
  human marking and evidence rules.

The holdout is currently blocked at zero certified papers. `paper-01` is the design paper
and cannot serve as holdout evidence; no holdout score may be reported until the
pre-registered number of certified papers exists.
